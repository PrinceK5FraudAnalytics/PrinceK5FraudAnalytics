# Prince Kumar

### AI Engineer | Machine Learning Researcher | Reproducible Systems Builder

I build practical AI systems and reproduce ideas from research papers into understandable, testable code. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, deep learning, language models, and agentic engineering.

## What I research

- **Transformer architectures** - implementation details, training dynamics, and efficient reproduction.
- **Language-model reasoning** - chain-of-thought, thought distillation, and merging reasoning from multiple outputs.
- **Large-to-small model alignment** - transferring useful reasoning behavior into smaller, efficient models.
- **Training as compilation** - teaching models to produce executable programs and structured solutions.
- **AI engineering systems** - agents, RAG, evaluation, optimization, local inference, and production workflows.
